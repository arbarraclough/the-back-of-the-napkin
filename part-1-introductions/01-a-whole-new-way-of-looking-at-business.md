# 1. A Whole New Way of Looking at Business

> "The heart of business is the art of problem solving."

> "Visual thinking means taking advantage of our innate ability to see-both with your eyes and with our mind's eye-in order to discover ideas that are otherwise invisible, develop those ideas quickly and intuitively, and then share those ideas with other people in a way that they simply 'get.'"

A simple "back of the napkin" sketch can:

1. Help you clarify vague ideas in your own mind.
2. Be drawn almost instantly, without the need for any technology.
3. Be shared with audiences in an open way that invites commends and discussions.
4. Be spoken about without the reliance on notes, bullet points, or a script.
