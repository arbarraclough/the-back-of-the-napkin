# 8. Showing and The Visual Thinking MBA

> After we've identified our problem, selected the appropriate showing framework, and further focused our ideas using the SQVID, the next step is to put pen to paper (or napkin or whiteboard) and start drawing.

Each of the chapters in _Part 3: Developing Ideas_ is a dedicated case study into each of the six visual frameworks.

> "While computers are insanely wonderful tools for countless applications, I can't think of anything that they add to visual thinking at this level - while I can think of several things they take away."
